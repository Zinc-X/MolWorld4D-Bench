# ComplexMD-Eval 0.2.0 validation on the fixed LogicMD 10-case panel

## Execution result

- Protocol: `protocol.full.yaml`
- Final output: `v0.2.0_final/`
- Cases/replicas: 10/10 completed
- Evaluation failures: 0
- Frames: 500 prediction and 500 reference frames per case, declared 1 ns spacing
- Runtime: 2 min 24.78 s wall time
- Peak RSS: 1,114,296 KB
- Scalar metrics: 172
- Fully covered scalar metrics: 126
- Output tables: 27 CSV files
- Figures: 14 PNG files

## Topology correction

Version 0.1 retained sparse PDB `CONECT` records and therefore evaluated ligand
bonds/angles in only one case. Version 0.2 detects incomplete bond topology and
augments it geometrically. All 10 cases now have complete-check status, 10--29
ligand bonds, and 13--45 ligand angles.

The strict `physical_valid_frame_rate` requires every checked bond and ligand
angle to pass in the same frame and has a panel median of 0.008. The separately
reported size-robust `physical_soft_valid_frame_rate` requires 99% of all bonds,
90% of ligand bonds, and 90% of ligand angles to pass, plus no severe clash; its
median is 0.909. Neither value should be compared directly with v0.1 because the
topology and validity definition changed.

## Advanced-module coverage

The following completed for all 10 cases:

- TICA/VAMP slow modes and saved projections;
- reference-fitted state occupancy and transition matrices;
- implied timescales, CK diagnostics, transition-event statistics, and FES;
- Ramachandran, chi1, ligand graph dihedrals, Rg, SASA, and DSSP;
- classified protein-ligand interaction proxies;
- standard and advanced physical-time windows;
- per-case moving-block and panel case-bootstrap uncertainty;
- prediction runtime and ns/day.

All 10 cases had enough state changes for transition statistics. Only 3/10 had
connected reference/prediction state paths sufficient for MFPT comparison; the
other seven are explicitly `partial`, not silently assigned a value.

## Correctly non-estimable from these inputs

- Water bridges/hydration: no solvent atoms were retained.
- Energy/temperature/pressure: no aligned observable CSV was supplied.
- Reference-normalized baseline/trust horizon: one reference replica per case.
- Charge/geometry-resolved salt bridges and hydrogen bonds: no formal charges or
  bonded hydrogens; proxy metrics are available and labelled.
- MD wall-clock speedup: prediction runtimes exist, but reference MD runtimes do
  not. Prediction throughput is available; speedup remains missing.

These are input limitations, not execution failures. `module_status.csv` and
`metric_coverage.csv` preserve them for downstream audit.
